Know the limits

A missing record is not a clean bill of health.

PermitPulse is a research aid. Records may be incomplete, historical, delayed, outside city jurisdiction, or missing inspections. “Completed” does not automatically mean a final inspection passed. Confirm material findings with the issuing authority and your inspector.
