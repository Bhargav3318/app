Legal, privacy and sources

Use the record. Verify the conclusion.

PermitTap is an independent research tool and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any city or permitting authority. It provides public records for informational purposes and does not provide legal, engineering, inspection, title, or safety advice.

Limits and responsibility

Records may be incomplete, delayed, historical, outside a city's jurisdiction, or missing inspection details. A missing result does not establish that work was permitted, compliant, safe, or absent. A status or completion date does not by itself prove that a final inspection passed. Verify important findings with the issuing authority and qualified professionals before relying on them.

Privacy

PermitTap has no accounts, analytics, advertising trackers, external fonts, or user database. Search text is sent from your browser to the selected city's public-data service for Austin, San Antonio, and Dallas. Houston searches the static snapshot hosted with this site. GitHub Pages and source authorities may process normal web-request information, such as IP address and browser headers, under their own policies.

Corrections

Use the official source link on each result to confirm or correct a government record. Website issues can be reported through the project's GitHub issue tracker.
